Privacy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This page explains what happens to your information when you visit noinest.com or ask us to keep you posted. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Who is responsible

NoïNest is run by its founder, Jawad Bellamine, in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. For any question about your data, write to hello@noinest.com.

What we collect

  • Your email address, only if you give it to us in the form.
  • Your agreement to receive news from us, with its date and time.

We do not ask for your name, your children's names or any other detail. This site uses no advertising and no tracking cookies.

Counting visits

We count visits to this site with the privacy-friendly analytics of our host, Replit: how many people visit, which pages they open and which site sent them. It uses no cookies, does not build a profile of you and does not identify you. We use these numbers only to understand whether people find the site.

Why we use it

Only to write to you about NoïNest: when our first creation is ready, and occasional news after that. We send a few messages only. We never sell or share your email for anyone else's marketing.

Who helps us

  • Brevo (France, European Union) keeps our mailing list and sends our emails, including the email that asks you to confirm.
  • Replit (United States) hosts this website and counts visits for us. Like any web host, it handles the technical data your browser sends when you open a page.

Your data may therefore be stored outside the United Arab Emirates, with providers bound by their own data protection commitments.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. When you do, we remove your email from our list within 30 days.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe at any time with the link at the bottom of every email.
  • Ask us to see, correct or delete your data by writing to hello@noinest.com. We answer within 30 days.

If we change this notice, we will update the date at the top of this page.
